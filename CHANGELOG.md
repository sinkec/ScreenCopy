# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-04)

Tested on the same setup as 0.1.0.

- Play a video file on the TV instead of a monitor, with pause and seeking, including 10 second steps.
- Play a playlist, which stays visible in the window, one file after another without a new connection.
- Show SRT subtitles on the picture, with size, position and delay that can change while watching.
- Choose among a file's sound tracks, and change the film's volume.
- Go on with a film from where it was left.
- Use the keyboard to pause, seek and change the volume, and the tray's menu to pause.
- Send films at a higher quality and at 60 frames per second by default, with a quality choice in Preferences.
- Connect again automatically when the connection to the TV is lost.
- Ask for the administrator password once while the program runs, while the firewall still opens only during each
  session. The password is no longer remembered by the system for a few minutes.
- Keep looking for the TV after a session, as a TV can need a few minutes before it can be found again.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-03)

First release. Tested on Linux Mint 22 (Cinnamon, X11) with NVIDIA graphics and a Samsung AU7172 TV.

- Mirror one chosen monitor to a Miracast TV, with sound.
- Choose the monitor from a picture of your real layout, including size, position and rotation.
- Find TVs automatically, and keep the last used TV at the top of the list.
- Remember which formats each TV supports, and offer only those.
- Open a running firewall only on the direct connection to the TV, and only during the session.
- Keep running in the tray, with Stop Sharing while sharing.
- Adjust the delay between sound and picture while watching.
- Choose a light or dark appearance.
- Record a monitor to a video file for testing without a TV.
