# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-03)

First release. Tested on Linux Mint 22 (Cinnamon, X11) with NVIDIA graphics and a Samsung AU7172 TV.

- Mirror one chosen monitor to a Miracast TV, with sound.
- Choose the monitor from a picture of your real layout, including size, position and rotation.
- Find TVs automatically, and keep the last used TV at the top of the list.
- Remember which formats each TV supports, and offer only those.
- Open a running firewall only on the direct connection to the TV, and only during the session.
- Keep running in the tray, with Stop Sharing while sharing.
- Adjust the delay between sound and picture while watching.
- Choose a light or dark appearance.
- Record a monitor to a video file for testing without a TV.
